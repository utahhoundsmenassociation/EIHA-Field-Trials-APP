🎲 Heat Tools:
Treeing Contest Score Entry

Each dog released for 30 seconds. Enter scores from all three judges — average auto-calculates (reported as 1-minute average).

Iron Dog Jackpot — Last Dog In Circle Wins

Last dog remaining in the circle wins the jackpot. Dogs are disqualified if they leave the circle. All entrants participate — jackpot paid to winner.

Big Bear Race Jackpot — 1st to Tree / 2nd to Tree

Individual scent drag race. 1st to tree wins. 50% to 1st place / 50% club.

Bear Races Jackpot Events

3-Dog Bear Race: All teams are assumed qualified. Check DNQ for any team that did not finish. Fastest qualifying time wins. Enter finish times in HH:MM:SS format.

Add Pack Team
Enter the team name, handler, pack size, and all dogs in this pack. All teams are assumed qualified. Check DNQ if a team did not finish.
